## 🧭 Navigating the App (Modules & Features)

### 💳 1. [ EXPENSES ] — Smart Expense & Money Ledger (TOP PRIORITY)
- **Daily Income & Expense Ledger**: Log your daily spending and salary/income with custom categories, dates, and notes.
- **Monthly Category Budgets & Crimson Red Alerts**: Set spending limits (Food, Room, Travel, Shopping). Exceeding a budget triggers prominent **Crimson Red** warning alerts across the interface.
- **Automatic Rolling Savings**: Leftover savings from the previous month automatically roll over into the new month's pool.
- **Wealth & Investments Hub**: Track your long-term SIP Mutual Funds, and optional company-matched Provident Fund (PF) and GMC Health Insurance.
- **1-Click Statement Export**: Download full reports in **Excel (.xlsx)**, **CSV**, or print a formatted **PDF statement**.

---

### 🍎 2. [ FOOD LOG ] — Nutrition & Daily Protein Tracker
- **Smart Indian Food Dictionary**: Pre-loaded nutritional profiles for **Milk, Sattu, Boiled Eggs, Chicken, Rice, Dalma, Paneer, Oats, Dates, Nuts, Poha**, and more.
- **Auto-Calculated Macros**: Enter your portion size (e.g., 100g, 2 eggs, 1 bowl) and it instantly calculates **Protein, Calories, Carbs, and Fats**.
- **Milk Auto-Fill Routine**: Searching `milk` offers 1-tap auto-fill for **₹10 Daily Pouch (200ml / 6.4g Prot)** and **Weekend Pouch (500ml / 16g Prot)**.
- **Sweet / Cheat Treat Alerts**: Flags high-sugar foods (>10g) with red warning alerts to keep your diet disciplined.

---

### 📖 3. [ LEARNING ] — Study Hours & Skill Roadmap Tracker
- **Daily Study Log**: Track daily study duration, subjects, and topics covered.
- **Visual Roadmap Milestones**: Build custom skill roadmaps with target completion dates and live progress bars.
- **Study Stopwatch**: Built-in persistent timer to time your deep focus sessions.

---

### 🤍 4. [ WELLNESS ] — Sleep Quality & Daily Habits
- **Sleep Quality Tracker**: Log bedtime, wake-up time, and sleep quality recovery scores.
- **Daily Hydration & Habits**: Track your daily water intake and habit consistency.

---

### ⊞ 5. [ DASHBOARD ] — Unified Growth Center
- **All-in-One Snapshot**: View Net Savings, Protein Intake, Study Hours, and Sleep Score on a single screen.
- **Consistency Streaks**: Visual rings and progress trackers to keep you disciplined every day.

- **100% Cloud Synced**: All data is stored in **MongoDB Atlas** with AES-256 encryption.
