# 🌟 Personal Labs — All-in-One Growth & Daily Life Tracker
> *A private, cloud-synced productivity suite for Money, Nutrition, Learning, Wellness, and Encrypted Chat.*

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (How to Access)
1. **Open the Website**: Open the link in your mobile browser or laptop.
2. **Create an Account**: Click **Register**, enter your Name, Email, and Password.
3. **Log in**: Sign in to immediately access your private dashboard. All your data is encrypted and synced across all your devices in real-time.

---

## 📱 What You Can Do (Key Features)

### 💰 1. Smart Expense & Money Ledger
- **Log Daily Transactions**: Track your daily Income and Expenses with date, amount, category, and notes.
- **Budget Limits & Red Alerts**: Set monthly budget limits for categories (e.g., Food, Room, Travel). If you exceed the budget, the system highlights it with a crimson red warning.
- **Rolling Net Savings**: Automatically rolls over your leftover savings from previous months into the new month.
- **Wealth, SIP & PF Hub**:
  - Track Mutual Funds (SIP) & Long-Term Investments.
  - Optional **EPFO Provident Fund & Health Insurance** calculator (auto-calculates company match & retirement corpus).
- **1-Click Statement Export**: Download full Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or print a formatted PDF statement for any month or custom date range.

---

### 🥗 2. Nutrition & Daily Protein Tracker
- **Smart Indian Food Dictionary**: Built-in nutrition database for Indian foods:
  - **Milk**: Auto-fills ₹10 Daily Pouch (200ml / 6.4g protein) or Weekend Pouch (500ml / 16g protein).
  - **Sattu, Eggs, Chicken, Rice, Dalma, Paneer, Dates, Nuts, Oats, Poha, etc.**
- **Automatic Macro Calculation**: Calculates Calories, Protein, Carbs, and Fats automatically based on your portion size.
- **Sweet / Cheat Treat Alerts**: Warns you if an item contains excess sugar so you stay on track with your fitness goals.

---

### 📚 3. Study & Skill Roadmap Tracker
- **Log Study Hours**: Track daily study duration, subjects, and topics covered.
- **Visual Learning Milestones**: Create custom roadmaps with target completion dates and progress tracking.

---

### 🌿 4. Wellness & Habit Routine
- **Sleep & Rest Tracker**: Log bedtime, wake-up time, and sleep quality scores.
- **Water & Daily Habit Logs**: Stay consistent with your daily hydration and wellness routines.

---

### 💬 5. Secret Peer-to-Peer Chat & Voice/Video Calls
- **Private Messaging**: Connect with friends using their email address.
- **Live Online Presence**: See who is online in real-time.
- **Instant Audio & Video Calls**: Free, peer-to-peer, encrypted voice and video calling directly from your browser without installing third-party apps.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Cloud Sync
- **100% Cloud Synced**: Add an expense or food log on your mobile phone, and it updates immediately on your laptop.
- **Complete Privacy**: All data is encrypted with AES-256 and isolated to your personal account.
